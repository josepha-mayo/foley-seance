## Fixed perspective versus game-engine response

Foley bakes its artistic movement, door transition and echo into stereo PCM. Replaying the file does not recompute those choices from a player position or world geometry. Its proposed use is a fixed-perspective short scene, cutscene or other preauthored cue; usefulness still needs creator/listener evaluation.

Epic documents listener-relative attenuation and spatialization, runtime obstruction checks, and stereo-source spread in [Unreal Engine Sound Attenuation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sound-attenuation-in-unreal-engine). Thus stereo itself is not an engine-compatibility disqualifier. Our inference is that applying further engine spatialization can change an already-authored stereo image and must be evaluated for the intended use; no Unreal/other-engine integration test has been performed. Do not claim that this export replaces interactive geometry/listener-dependent sound behavior.
