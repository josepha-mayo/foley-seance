## Release review

Use a Foley-only tree, never publish the mixed private workbench. Reconcile it against the exact final code checkpoint and rerun tests/build from a clean source copy. The build-output guard rejects unexpected old files rather than deleting them. Review every included file and history for secrets, private recordings, unrelated projects and third-party rights. Confirm `.kiro` is included, and verify public links in a signed-out session after authorized publication. Keep source-safe Kiro history, distinguish generated drafts from integration work, and do not fabricate unused Kiro features.

## Competitive presentation

Show one useful sound-design task from dry source to a deliberately shaped cue, then save/reopen and export. Explain the target creator, the friction removed and what the haunted controls do. Do not pitch novel DSP, professional acoustic simulation, production certification or listener validation without evidence. Do not present visual doors or their automation as inventions: Audio Ease documents them in the [Indoor manual](https://audioease.com/indoor/files/Indoor-five-page-manual.pdf) and [official release notes](https://audioease.com/indoor/). These are vendor descriptions, not a hands-on comparison. Demonstrate the short horror-cue workflow instead. The five equally weighted judging areas are impact, implementation/Kiro, design, idea and presentation; test counts alone demonstrate none of their human-facing outcomes.
